# B10.3 — fragment stitch, record grouping, record style

Fourth batch of the five day repair cycle, and the first of it that reaches the
translation path. The reductions of §W (W-B10-01..04) apply. Five samples, whole
documents, target pages written out.

What landed: a pass that puts a written unit the paragraph finder left in pieces
back together before the translator sees it (T1, T5), a line structure pass that
reads how many records a block holds instead of assuming one per line (T2), and
a record that is set the way its own characters are set instead of the way its
parent mostly was (T3, T4).

Unlike the two batches before it this one is **not** a free replay. Three of its
changes reach the request text and the first run paid 119 API calls for them.
§7 is the bill.

## 2. T1 — the stitch, and the two guards the plan did not state

`babeldoc/magazine/fragment_stitch.py`, `configs/fragment_stitch.json`, switch
`magazine_fragment_stitch`, run from the page kind hook immediately before the
line structure pass. A page whose declared policy raises
`preserve_line_structure` is exempt whole, as the plan specifies.

Two rules admit a pair. **Inline** is two pieces side by side on one line: their
line bands overlap by `stitch_min_y_overlap_ratio` (0.6) of the shorter and the
space between them is no wider than `stitch_max_inline_gap_ratio` (0.8) of their
type size. **Vertical** is one piece above another in one column, on the
fragment census detector's own keys: horizontal overlap at least
`stitch_min_x_overlap_ratio` (0.5) of the narrower and vertical space no deeper
than `stitch_max_line_gap_ratio` (1.5) of their median height. Inline runs
first and vertical over what inline produced, which is the order Courier-en p4
needs: neither row overlaps the other horizontally until each row is whole.

### The unit guard, and why the rules as written could not ship

The plan states the two rules as geometry plus a style tolerance. Measured over
the two target pages, that admits **ten** pairs on Courier-en p4 and **twelve**
on CERNCourier-en p3, and almost all of them are consecutive paragraphs of
ordinary running text:

| pair | left ends | right begins | wanted? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Courier-en p4 `#4`→`#5` | `…of how t` | `raditional knowledge` | yes |
| Courier-en p4 `#6`→`#7` | `…as diverse as` | `water management,` | yes |
| Courier-en p4 `#3`→`#4` | `…in laboratory studies.` | `There are many more…` | **no** |
| Courier-en p4 `#8`→`#9` | `…pattern language.` | `These practices represent…` | **no** |
| Courier-en p4 `#9`→`#10` | `…Today, Indigenous` | `communities worldwide…` | **no** (a column break, and column reflow is out of scope) |
| CERN p3 `#34`→`#35` | `…three main objectives:` | `Preserving the integrity…` | **no** |

Geometry cannot separate these, because every column of running text is made of
boxes near each other. So a **unit guard** was added, and both halves of it are
needed: the left piece may not end its sentence, and one of the two pieces must
be short enough by `stitch_max_fragment_chars` (40, the census detector's own
bound) to be a piece rather than a paragraph. The first refuses `#3`→`#4` and
`#8`→`#9`; the second refuses `#9`→`#10` and `#34`→`#35`. Neither alone refuses
all four. Both are declared, both are asserted live in the gate
(`check_02b`), and the deviation is registered as **D3** in §9.

### The face is read by name, not by id

The plan says "same face". Implemented as the same font *id*, CERNCourier-en
p3's first stranded initial does not stitch: `T`/`h` carry `TT3` and the body
carries `C2_0`, and both are `FIAIXI+MerriweatherLight`. A font id is scoped to
the resource dictionary it is reached through — the same page maps `C2_0` to
`MerriweatherLight` inside one form and to `Saira-Bold` inside another — so an
id comparison is not a comparison of typefaces at all. The pass resolves the
name through the page's and each form's font tables. Registered as **D4**.

### A defect found by running it

The first run stitched Courier-en p4 correctly and printed it at **half size**.
A stitch leaves its merged-away members in place holding nothing, which is the
batch's own hard constraint, and the first implementation left their *boxes*
alone as well. The typesetting stage trims a paragraph's lower edge back to
clear whatever paragraph stands below it (`typesetting.render_page`), and four
blanked members standing inside the merged unit's own band are exactly such
paragraphs: the unit was trimmed from 155 pt of height to 22 pt and scaled to
0.50 to fit. `_blank` now clears the box as well, the unit lays out at 0.95, and
the gate asserts the invariant on stubs (`check_02a`) and over every blanked
member of every run (`check_06a2`, twelve of them).

## 3. T5 — the dropped initial rule accepts nothing, and that is the measurement

The rule is implemented as the plan states it: a cluster of at most
`initial_max_chars` (3) characters at the upper left of the paragraph beside it,
set at `initial_min_font_ratio` (1.6) times that paragraph's size.

It accepts **nothing** on this corpus, and the reason is on the record for every
candidate rather than inferred. The fragments F2's defect A7 names are not set
larger than the body at all:

| sample | page | fragment | body opens | size ratio | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CERNCourier-en | 3 | `h` | `e European Strategy reiterates…` | 1.0 | refused, below 1.6 |
| CERNCourier-en | 3 | `h` | `e Strategy process has seen…` | 1.0 | refused, below 1.6 |
| CERNCourier-en | 4 | `C` | `CJulAug26_HI` | 1.0 | refused, below 1.6 |
| CERNCourier-en | 4 | `C` | `CJulAug26_HI` | 1.0 | refused, below 1.6 |
| AramcoWorld-en-v2 | 7 | `Sea` | `Madinah` | 1.0 | refused, below 1.6 |

A7 is a *word* broken into boxes, not an initial set large and left behind. What
closes both CERN p3 cases is the **inline** rule, which does not read size at
all; the pixels are in §6. The rule is kept rather than deleted because the shape
it describes is real and 1.6 is the right bound for it, and its refusals are
filed with the figure that refused them so a later corpus can be measured against
it. No knob was moved to make it accept something. Registered as **D5**.

## 4. T2 — record grouping, and the eight pages it reaches

Inside one paragraph the distances between consecutive lines are measured and
their median taken; a distance of at least `record_gap_ratio` (1.6, ranged
1.1..5.0) of that median is a record boundary. A paragraph in which no distance
reaches the bound has one peak and no boundary in it, and there the line is the
record — which is what the pass did before this batch. A paragraph in which some
distance does is cut into the groups the distances mark, each group one
paragraph in its own band, and the lines inside a group are not cut apart.

The judgement is the printer's own mechanism — records are set apart and the
lines inside one are set close — measured against the paragraph's own leading,
so the same figures at another leading give the same answer. Nothing page level
is read. The gate asserts both branches on stubs and, over the corpus, that a
paragraph reports more than one record if and only if some distance reached the
bound (`check_04c`).

| sample | page | kind | paragraphs | cut | regrouped | lines → records | restyled | exempt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Courier-en | 1 | toc | 50 | 12 | **0** | 27 → 27 | 15 | 7 |
| Vogue-en | 3 | toc | 61 | 11 | 5 | 66 → 40 | 9 | 11 |
| CERNCourier-en | 2 | toc | 127 | 18 | 2 | 71 → 61 | 54 | 8 |
| AramcoWorld-en-v2 | 3 | toc | 21 | 0 | 0 | — | 0 | 5 |
| FD-en-v2 | 3 | toc | 52 | 8 | 0 | 23 → 23 | 5 | 5 |
| AramcoWorld-en-v2 | 2 | masthead | 25 | 6 | 3 | 36 → 19 | 11 | 3 |
| FD-en-v2 | 5 | masthead | 61 | 7 | 0 | 21 → 21 | 7 | 7 |
| FD-en-v2 | 7 | masthead | 33 | 0 | 0 | — | 0 | 6 |

The two contents pages the plan asked to be put in front of a ruling —
AramcoWorld p3 and FD p3 — are the two the grouping does not move at all: on
AramcoWorld p3 the bounds cut nothing in the first place, and on FD p3 every
block is evenly leaded, so every record is a line exactly as before. There is
nothing to rule on. What did move on those samples is on their *masthead* pages,
which the same flag declares.

**Courier-en p1 is the page the branch is anchored on.** Every one of its twelve
cut blocks is evenly leaded: the widest distance ratio anywhere on it is 1.22,
against a bound of 1.6. So the record account is byte for byte the account F2
produced — same twelve blocks, same line counts, same exempt set — and the gate
asserts it (`check_04d`). See D2 for what that claim is *not*.

## 5. T3 and T4 — a record set as its own characters are set

A paragraph's base style is what `ILTranslator.get_translate_input` hands the
translator and what the translated text is laid out in. A record inheriting its
parent's base style is set the way that parent mostly was, and on a contents page
mostly bylines that prints every entry title at byline size. Each record now
takes the majority style of its own visible characters (T3); a stitched unit
takes the majority of its members and its minority characters are moved to it,
so the unit is one style run rather than a rich text request (T4).

One hundred and one records across the corpus are set differently as a result. It is the
most visible change of the batch and CERNCourier-en p2 is where to see it. In F2
the Features block reads as one undifferentiated small column —
`历史 / 当粒子 / 走向全球 / 对……的反思 / 第四届国际 / 粒子历史研讨会`. Here the
title is set at display size and the blurb small under it:
`历史 / **当粒子走向全球** / 关于的思考 …`, and the same for
`**束流模拟的瑞士军刀**`, `**在缺失能量前沿的十年**`, `**宇宙射线从极地到极地**`.
Rasters: `examples/output/F2/raster/CERNCourier-en.p2.png` against
`examples/output/b10_3/CERNCourier-en/raster/CERNCourier-en.p2.png`.

The gate does not read the count back from the report. It recomputes, from the
run's own checkpoint, that every record of the document carries the majority
style of the characters inside it (`check_05b`, 61 records on CERNCourier-en).

## 7. §Cost — the bill, and why a replay is not the bill

The batch changes request text in three ways, all intended: a stitch replaces
several requests with one, record grouping replaces several with one, and a
record's own base style changes which style placeholders its request carries,
because a placeholder is written exactly where a run differs from the base style.

**First run**, every changed request unseen by the project cache:

| sample | API calls | prompt tok | completion tok |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vogue-en | 13 | 10 855 | 1 766 |
| CERNCourier-en | 56 | 59 537 | 7 784 |
| Courier-en | 12 | 15 907 | 2 576 |
| AramcoWorld-en-v2 | 26 | 33 495 | 6 085 |
| FD-en-v2 | 12 | 23 448 | 512 |
| **total** | **119** | **143 242** | **18 723** |

The plan estimated "under one hundred". Measured 119, and it is reported rather
than rounded (**D6**).

**Replay**, the run this report's evidence comes from: 9 API calls in total,
and they are the repair loop's — CERNCourier-en 2 and AramcoWorld-en-v2 7. The
translator sent nothing the cache could not answer.

### The request account

Requests are compared as texts rather than by position, because a run that
replaces several requests with one does not line up by index with the run before
it. Every request is either F2's or on the whitelist, and both directions of the
whitelist are written down in each sample's `parity.json`.

| sample | F2 | b10.3 | unchanged | withdrawn | introduced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vogue-en | 74 | 42 | 32 | 42 | 10 |
| CERNCourier-en | 230 | 220 | 197 | 33 | 23 |
| Courier-en | 134 | 130 | 118 | 16 | 12 |
| AramcoWorld-en-v2 | 166 | 149 | 139 | 27 | 10 |
| FD-en-v2 | 161 | 161 | 160 | 1 | 1 |
| **total** | **765** | **702** | **646** | **119** | **56** |

`answers_changed` — a request whose text is in both runs and whose answer is not
— reads 9, 34, 9, 35, 0. That figure is **not** model non-determinism, and the
field is no longer called `resampled` for that reason. The unit the translator
caches is the batch it sends, not the paragraph: several paragraphs of a page go
into one prompt, so a batch holding one paragraph this pass changed misses the
cache as a whole and every paragraph in it is answered again with its own text
unchanged. That is why the figure is several times the API call count rather
than equal to it.

## 8. Conservation, the double write, and the `unicode` readers

**Paragraph count.** A stitch removes nothing and renumbers nothing: the members
are merged into the first of them and the rest stay where they stood, so a
reference minted before the pass still resolves after it. Page counts are
identical to F2 on every sample. On the eight declared pages the count changes
by the split, and the account closes: cut blocks + records + exempt paragraphs
reconcile per page in `line_split.report.json`.

**The double write.** Every stitched paragraph's `unicode` is written from the
same characters its composition was rebuilt from, so the two cannot drift apart
here. The gate recomputes it from each run's checkpoint (`check_06b`, seven
paragraphs). B10.1's divergence is not added to.

**What reads `unicode`, as prerequisite 6 asks.** One reader, reached by all of
them: `magazine/reading_order.py::paragraph_reading_text` returns the
composition's own characters in reading order and falls back to `unicode` **only
where a paragraph carries no composition at all**. Every detector goes through
`detectors/base.rendered_text`, which is that function; so does the repair path.
The fallback is therefore the whole exposure, and after this batch it is reached
by exactly the paragraphs a stitch blanked — whose `unicode` is `""`, which is
what they show. No detector reads `unicode` directly.

## 9. Deviations from the plan

Eleven, all reported rather than absorbed. D3, D4 and D9 are changes to what was
built; the rest are measurements that came out other than the plan predicted.

1. **D1 — the census reaches 4, not 0** (§6). No knob was moved; the assertion
   was replaced by a stronger property that holds across the corpus.
2. **D2 — Courier-en p1 is not byte identical to the run before it, and cannot
   be.** The plan asserts both that T3 recomputes the style of every record and
   that this page's output is byte identical to the baseline. The two are
   mutually exclusive: fifteen of that page's records are set differently on
   purpose, which is the batch's most visible improvement there — a title record
   that inherited a byline-heavy parent's 7 pt style is now set at its own 10 pt.
   What *is* unchanged, and what the gate asserts, is the **record account**:
   same twelve blocks, same line counts, same exempt set, and its translation
   requests unchanged except for the placeholder consequences of the restyling.
   Separately, there is no b10.2 run of Courier-en to compare against — b10.2 ran
   three samples, none of them this one — so the comparison is against F2, which
   is the frozen reference both later batches used.
3. **D3 — the two rules needed a unit guard the plan does not state** (§2).
   Without it the geometry joins whole columns.
4. **D4 — "same face" is same font *name*, not same font id** (§2).
5. **D5 — the dropped initial rule accepts nothing** (§3).
6. **D6 — 119 API calls against an estimate of "under 100"** (§7).
7. **D7 — the conservation assertion as written is not reachable.** The plan's
   assertion 5 requires paragraph counts equal to F2 per page except on declared
   pages. Declared pages are where the split happens, so the exception is the
   whole of the claim; what is asserted instead is the closed account per page.
8. **D8 — the reach is eight pages, not five** (§4). `preserve_line_structure`
   is declared on `masthead` too.
9. **D9 — a blanked member must give up its box** (§2), found by running it.
10. **D10 — a vertical join inherits the pipeline's own spacing rule.** The
    merged text is written by `layout_helper.get_char_unicode_string`, which
    inserts a space between two characters only where their boxes say a line
    ended. On AramcoWorld p7 the two lines of a tight map label overlap
    vertically by their ascenders, so `Mediterranean` and `Sea` join without a
    space. Upstream behaviour, not touched: the same function writes every
    paragraph's text in the pipeline, and changing it is an upstream change this
    cycle does not make.
11. **D11 — CERNCourier-en p4's printing slug is stitched.** Its two halves are
    labelled `fallback_line` and `title`, both inside the declared label
    vocabulary, so the pass repairs the broken text as it would any other. The
    slug is furniture the layout parser labelled as text; nothing on the page
    moves, and the merge is left rather than special-cased away.

  conservative one and it held.

  paragraph, recorded, not added to. §8 lists what could read it.
- **The map label spacing** of D10.
