## 2. T1 — the policy matrix

`configs/translation_style.json` now declares four policies that state something
(`transliterate`, `translate`, `keep`, `annotate`) and one that states nothing
(`keep_source`), each with a whole role text per declared target language, each
pinned by the SHA-256 of its bytes. The loader refuses a text whose digest has
moved, which is what makes "frozen" a property rather than a claim.

The selected default is `transliterate` and its two texts are byte for byte the
ones every run since B9.1 was built with. The proof is not a comment: the gate
compiles the default for each target language and compares the digest against
what the frozen F2 ledger recorded per run — `74666d71…` for `zh`, `6f5e4231…`
for `en` (`check_02b`).

Every text ends by stating that a glossary table outranks it, `keep` included and
in as many words. A ruled name is a decision about one name and a policy is a
default about all of them; the model is the only thing that can honour that
ordering, so it is written where the model reads it.

are disjoint from the brackets `paren_dedup` folds. The gate builds the exact
shape the fold eats — a parenthetical repeating the text in front of it — in both
bracket sets, and asserts the fold takes the one and not the other
(`check_02e`). Behaviour-level validation of `keep` and `annotate` is explicitly
out of this batch; the gate reaches the compilation layer and stops.

## 5. T3b — the horizontal rule on a page whose lines are records

### The audit

`babeldoc/magazine/source_audit.py`, with `tools/source_audit.py` as its command
line. Deterministic, no model. It reads the page twice — the pipeline's own
intermediate language, and PyMuPDF's word extractor — and uses only where they
disagree.

Three findings changed the design while it was being built, each from running it:

* Banding by the intermediate language's **line** boxes puts two rows of two
  columns in one band: a paragraph beginning at the end of one row and running
  into the next carries a single line box eighteen points tall. Banding is by
  **character**.
* Banding by the **inked** character box splits the letters of a word: an `a` and
  a `p` on one baseline differ by the descender. Banding is by the **em** box.
* A band's verdict is not a fragment's verdict. A rotated credit rail crossing a
  band is a word the independent reading files there and the pipeline files on
  its own line, which made whole bands read as `dropped_text`. A fragment is
  placed by its own span through the **alignment**, and the two directions of
  disagreement are kept apart: characters the pipeline holds and the extractor
  does not are the fragment's business; the reverse is reported and charged to
  nobody.

**Branch: A only.** No duplicate layer anywhere in the corpus, no dropped text, no
extraction order fault. The B branch (blank the surplus) is implemented and
asserted on a stub, because the reason it exists is that stitching a duplicate

An earlier, looser B criterion produced 43 false positives across three samples —
the CERN digital edition's chrome bar draws each glyph twice *within* one
paragraph, and a band whose independent reading is empty made every character
look surplus. Both are now excluded: a duplicate needs its text to occur in
another paragraph of the same band, and a band with no independent reading places
nothing.

## 6. T5 — the display cut

Three levels, each falling softly back to the next. The corpus holds exactly one
display line chain, on Courier-en p2→p3.

```
source:  "How Indigenous knowledge drives" | "scientific discover y"
joint:   土著知识如何推动科学发现   (12 characters)

b10.3, by source share:   31/51 -> cut 7 -> 土著知识如何推 | 动科学发现
b10.4, by measured length: 8/12 -> cut 8 -> 土著知识如何推动 | 科学发现
```

Two auxiliary requests, one per member, cached and at temperature zero. **Only
the length is used.** The auxiliary text is discarded inside the method and never
returned: what leaves it is a list of integers, and the chain's output comes from
the joint translation and from nothing else. The sidecar carries both lengths,
the source shares it would otherwise have used, and the estimate per cut — and
carries no auxiliary text, which the gate asserts in both directions
(`check_05c`).

The second level moves an estimate onto a nearby clause end within
`cut_snap_radius` (2, ranged 0..4): after a punctuation mark, or after one of the
profile's `cut_boundary_markers`. The Chinese profile declares eighteen
closed-class words; the Latin profile declares **none**, because its break rule
already snaps to word boundaries. That is the direction negative, and it is
structural rather than measured (`check_05b`).

With `magazine_chain_cut_align` down the cascade starts at the third level and
reproduces b10.3's placement exactly (`check_05a`).
