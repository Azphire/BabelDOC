## 2. Overturning a B9.5 decision, in full

B9.5 declared `resolve_collision` **inert** and asserted it. That decision is
reversed here, and the argument is not that B9.5 was wrong.

**What B9.5 argued.** The causes of an overlap are not one cause and their
answers are opposite. A display headline painted twice in the source wants one
layer suppressed; a caption grown into the column beside it wants moving or
resetting; a folio printed inside the entry it numbers wants nothing at all,
because it was always there and the entry moved. Moving a paragraph blindly
costs more than leaving the overlap, because an overlap is legible under
inspection and a paragraph moved to the wrong place is not recoverable from the
finished page at all.

That reasoning is untouched. What has gone is the two conditions it stood on.

**Condition one: nothing separated the causes.** Overlap was measured by the
ratio over the union alone, which on this corpus reported almost nothing, and
what it did report it reported without asking whether the source had drawn it.
Both are now false. The coverage measure finds the overlaps the union ratio
cannot see, and the source exemption — now also read by coverage — removes the
largest and most dangerous class before the action is shown anything. This batch
measured the size of that class directly: of the **26** candidate pairs on the
four target pages, **25 are overlaps the source itself drew** and are exempted,
and **one** reaches the action. Under B9.5's iou-only exemption those 25 were
not exempt; they were merely never candidates, and the same verdict came out by
a different road.

**Condition two: there was nowhere to judge a finding one at a time.** B9.7's
per-kind rounds are that place. A heterogeneous cause is answered in a decision
about a particular finding, which is where heterogeneity belongs, rather than by
an action refusing its whole kind in advance.

**And the heterogeneity is still honoured, by narrowing rather than by
rhetoric.** The action refuses every pair it cannot name one member of as the
one to move: the smaller box's area over the larger's must be at or below
`resolve_max_area_ratio` (0.5). Two comparable blocks — where moving either is
exactly as wrong as moving the other — are escalated. That is precisely the
fixture B9.5's acceptance was measured on, and it is still refused. The
supersession is registered as **W-B10-06**: B9.5's `check_04f` keeps every
substantive claim (nothing written, document byte identical, refusal recorded,
stop reason unchanged) and only its reason constant moves, from
`REASON_REPORT_ONLY` to `REASON_AREA`.

## 3. T1 — the coverage criterion

`collision_min_coverage` (0.4) and `collision_source_min_coverage` (0.4), both
ranged. A pair is a candidate at or above **either** bound, and exempt as the
source's design at or above **either** source bound.

The measure exists because the union ratio divides by an area neither box
occupies alone. A drop cap standing inside its own body paragraph shares the
whole of its own area and about 0.2% of the union: `iou 0.0017`, `coverage
0.7876`. Over this corpus that shape is the common one.

Every exemption is now filed per pair with the route that took it, because
widening a bound moves pairs *between* routes as well as into and out of the
finding set, and a record holding only the verdict cannot show that the verdict
survived.

### Predicted against measured

Prediction is the new formula applied to the figures B9.5 froze. Measurement is
each run's own detection before it repaired anything, which is identical in the
two arms; the detect arm — detection with the loop switched off, spending
nothing — supplies the unrepaired page the pixel comparison needs.

| sample | pair | census iou / coverage | source coverage | predicted | measured |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AramcoWorld-en-v2 | `p3#0`/`p3#4` | 0.0089 / 0.5234 | 0.1347 | raised | **raised**, 0.0089 / 0.5234, route `coverage` |
| CERNCourier-en | `p3#11`/`p3#13` | 0.0016 / 0.7876 | 1.0 | source exempt | **exempt**, route `source_coverage` |
| CERNCourier-en | `p3#22`/`p3#24` | 0.0052 / 0.8184 | 1.0 | source exempt | **exempt**, route `source_coverage` |
| CERNCourier-en | `p4#41`/`p4#44` (slug) | — / 1.0 | 1.0 | source exempt | **exempt**, route `source_coverage` |
| Vogue-en | `p3#18`/`p3#19` | 0.1031 / 0.5458 | 0.5144 | source exempt | **not a candidate** |

Totals on the four target pages: **1 finding, 25 source exemptions, 0 false
positives** (AramcoWorld p3 1/0, Vogue p3 0/3, CERNCourier p3+p4 0/22). Every
CERN printing slug stays exempt, which prerequisite 3 required.

Two of these rows are results the plan did not expect, and both are reported
rather than tuned away.

**Three of the four named pairs are source design, not defects.** The plan's
gate assertion expected four findings. Three of the four are overlaps the source
already drew — two of them a drop cap standing *wholly inside* its body
paragraph, `source_coverage` 1.0000 — and raising them would report the
designer's decision as a fault, which is the one thing the source comparison
exists to prevent. The plan's four-finding expectation and its own
`collision_source_min_coverage` default are mutually inconsistent against the
frozen census; the knob is right and the expectation was drawn from a model in
which the exemption stayed iou-only. **No knob was changed to make the expected
number come out.**

**The Vogue pair is no longer a candidate at all**, and the cause is on the
record rather than inferred: `title_typeset` — a pass that did not exist when
the census was taken — sets `p3#18` as a single line at scale **0.6822** after
typesetting, and the smaller ink drops the pair under the bound. The gate
asserts both the absence and that stated cause, so a drift nobody accounted for
cannot later hide behind an explanation that has stopped being true.

Each exempted pair records `source_iou` below 0.05, which is the direct evidence
that the old exemption would *not* have taken it — the route really did change.

## 4. T2 — the action, and what it does

Slides the smaller member clear of the larger. Bounded by `resolve_max_area_ratio`
(0.5), `resolve_max_shift_ratio` (0.08 of the page along the axis travelled) and
`resolve_margin` (0.05 below the detector's bound). Mechanism, snapshot,
conservation and rollback are the containment action's.

**The guard is not.** Containment asks whether its repair would stand a
paragraph on something by the union ratio, because that was all an overlap meant
when it was written. Here that would be a hole: this action exists because the
union ratio cannot see a small box inside a large one, so a guard reading it
alone would slide a folio out of one entry and into the next without noticing.
The guard asks the same either-measure question the detector asks.

### The applied case

| | |
| --- | --- |
| pair | AramcoWorld-en-v2 `p3#0` / `p3#4` |
| mover | `p3#4` (area ratio 0.0172) |
| axis, distance | y, 1.3875 pt (bound 64.56 pt) |
| coverage | 0.5234 → 0.35 (target 0.35, detector bound 0.4) |
| outcome | **resolved** — absent from the run's final findings, not merely reduced |
| conservation | conserved; pages, paragraph counts and every untouched paragraph identical |
| new `out_of_page` | none |

Measured in pixels off the two arms' rendered pages: 470 pixels differ, and the
bounding box of every one of them is `(429, 68)–(478, 84)`, against the moved
paragraph's own rendered rect of `(429, 65)–(478, 83)`. Ink moved, and nothing
else on the page did.

The pixel assertion the plan asked for — that ink *overlap* falls — is not the
one made, and the reason is stated rather than worked around: the repair the
criterion selected is a slide of 1.39 pt, which is two pixels at 110 dpi, so an
overlap-count claim would be a claim about raster noise. What two pixels can
carry is the failure worth catching — a repair that rewrites boxes and moves no
glyph — and that is what is asserted.

### Two escape geometries, and a bug found by running it

The first implementation read the current overlap as the distance to travel.
That is right for two boxes meeting edge to edge and wrong for the case this
action mostly sees: a small box wholly inside a large one overlaps by its own
whole width however far it moves, until its trailing edge clears. The first
probe slid the box 3.51 pt and resolved nothing — coverage stayed at 1.0. The
distance is now solved from the edges, over both axes and both directions, and
the cheapest of the four is taken.

## 5. A rule the model could not read

The action's applicability was first written as the detector's disjunction:
two bounds, either sufficient. The rule is stated to the deciding model under
the heading that **all** stated conditions must hold, so a disjunction spread
over two conjoined sentences is a thing to be misread — and it was, on the first
request ever put in front of a model:

> The finding does not meet the conditions for 'resolve_collision' as both
> coverage (0.5234) and iou (0.0089) are below the required thresholds.

0.5234 is not below 0.4. The action never fired.

The bound was then shown to be unreachable rather than merely awkward. Coverage
is never below the union ratio, so a pair the ratio admits at 0.2 while coverage
refuses it at 0.4 is forced into a narrow region — and in that region the two
boxes are within 0.7185 of each other's area, which `resolve_max_area_ratio`
refuses at 0.5. Verified by construction over that region and asserted in the
gate (`check_03g`). The rule is now one sentence:

> its evidence reports coverage at or above 0.4

and the same model reads it correctly: *"The finding meets the condition with
coverage at 0.5234, which is above the required 0.4."*

`prompts/react_repair_decide.md` is unchanged, `94f39004…`, as the plan
required.

## 6. T3 — the decision cache

The key is taken over a **projection** of the request rather than the request.
`issues_block` renders twice: what is sent (unchanged, every field) and what is
hashed (the fields in `volatile_evidence_keys` dropped). Both request points —
the decision round and the orphan translation — go through one shared module,
`react/cache_key.py`, and `CACHE_KEY_VERSION` moved to 2.

`volatile_evidence_keys` is `["debug_id", "debug_ids", "source_checkpoint"]`.
The plan's illustrative initial value was `["debug_id"]` alone; the other two
are the collision kind's own run-varying fields — a pair of freshly minted ids
and a path under the working directory — and leaving them in would have left
exactly the kind this batch adds still missing on every run.

**Blacklist, not whitelist**, and the plan's own reasoning is recorded in the
module: a key still carrying a volatile field misses a cache it should have hit,
which costs one request and shows up in the ledger; a key that has dropped a
deciding field hits a cache it should have missed and replays a decision taken
about other evidence with nothing anywhere saying so. The first is visible and
recoverable, the second is neither, so a field nobody has classified stays in
the key.

**The translator cache is out of scope, and the plan's rider anchor is void.**
B10.1 had already attributed the persistent misses away from the translator:
`translate_tracking.json` holds only `page`, `cross_page` and `cross_column`,
every translator request is byte identical to F2's, and the misses are the repair
loop's. The rider asked to use those misses to validate a translator-side fix;
they cannot, because they are the very thing T3 repairs. The genuine second site
is `translate_orphan_lines`, which shares the composition and is fixed with it.

`api_calls 0`, `api_attributions` empty, every decision round served from the
cache, and `applications 1` — the same repair, for nothing. Before this batch
that run paid on every replay and would have paid forever.

## 10. What the sweep caught

The first clean sweep failed six assertions across four gates. Every one was a
real consequence of this batch, and each was fixed at the cause rather than by
relaxing the assertion. Two are worth recording beyond the fix.

**A backward compatibility break, and it was the serious one.** Making
`volatile_evidence_keys` a required declaration, and requiring the collision
rule to carry the coverage bound, made every configuration an earlier batch
froze unparseable. That is not a failing test; it is a capability lost. The
b9.6 and b9.7 gates reproduce the request b9.5 sent by parsing *b9.5's own
frozen configuration* -- reading today's tree instead would be circular -- so a
parser that refuses last year's file makes last year's run unreplayable, and
every future rule change would break it again. Both are now readable: the
declaration is optional and empty where absent, which is also the conservative
reading of a blacklist, and the collision rule may declare either era's overlap
bound while a rule declaring neither is still refused. The action reads only the
current term and refuses under the older one rather than reading a bound that
measures something else.

The same applies to the key itself. The version bump retires old keys by design,
which means the keys b9.5 filed no longer recompute. Overwriting the expected
values would have made those replays assert only that today's code agrees with
itself, so the composition version is now a parameter and the replays pin
version 1 -- the assertion is preserved exactly, and what it says is still that
the request b9.5 sent is reproduced here.

**A pre-existing anchoring bug in two gates.** `spec_check_b9_6` assertion 01b
and `spec_check_b9_7` assertion 01b each state a property of their own batch --
"only a description changed", "the vocabulary did not move" -- and read the live
working tree to check it. Such an assertion turns false the moment any later
batch legitimately edits the same file, which is a gate failing for having been
overtaken rather than for a fault. Both now anchor on their own batch tag, which
is what CLAUDE.md section 5 already asks of changed-file assertions.

The remaining three: newline. Editing `configs/` and `spec_checks/` files
through Python on Windows rewrote them CRLF, and `.gitattributes` pins those
trees `eol=lf` precisely because gates digest their bytes. Fifteen files
normalised back. Beside them, `spec_check_b9_5` lost a constant this batch
deleted and now names the bound the rule declares, and `spec_check_e0` learned
that this gate runs after it, registered in its own exception table with the
reason it asks for.

Zero regression on the substance: `spec_check_b8_4` **32/32**, the
nineteen-finding spectrum unchanged.
