# b11.13 — pulling `returned_unchanged` and `A\B` apart

Baseline commit `89904d6` (`b11.12-full-baseline`), branch
`migration/minimal-v0.6.4`. Census instrument extended at this batch's task C
commit; the six run artifacts are the b11.12 ones, unchanged.

This was an analysis. No pipeline code, no `configs/` parameter, no
`sample_matrix.json` and no `expectations/*.json` was changed. The census
gained two descriptive fields, `tracking_input_excerpt` and
`tracking_output_excerpt`, which no criterion reads; every count in the b11.12
report reproduces exactly.

---

## 0. The criteria, fixed before the data was read

Committed before any record was opened, git blob `a313027`:

**`returned_unchanged`**

- `correct_preservation` — the source is a proper noun, brand, publication
  title or abbreviation, **and** the accepted form in the target language is
  the source form itself. Handing it back unchanged is the right output.
- `should_have_translated` — the source is ordinary translatable wording: a
  phrase, clause or common noun with a normal target-language rendering.
- `undecided` — not decidable from the excerpt.

**`A\B`** (the detector reports residue; the ledger holds the paragraph
translated)

- `detection_false_positive` — the tracking output differs from the input, so
  a real translation happened, **and** the residual source-script run is a
  preserved name, brand or abbreviation inside an otherwise translated
  paragraph.
- `ledger_wrong` — the paragraph's output is not actually in the target
  language; the residue is the body of the text rather than a preserved name.
- `undecided` — not decidable.

Tie-break, declared in advance: where both readings survive the excerpt, the
record is `undecided`. A class is not forced to make a table look finished.

## 1. The two questions turned out to be one

All 13 `returned_unchanged` records are inside `A\B`. The set is not two


Plan 10 asked these as separate questions because b11.12 §6 and §12 named them
separately. They are the same 31 paragraphs seen from two directions, and the
answer to the second contains the answer to the first.

### Why the three are `undecided` and not forced

Rows 3, 4 and 13 carry no readable prose: two are a printer's timestamp whose
digits are placeholder markup, one is placeholder markup end to end. Neither
declared criterion fits — they are not proper nouns, and calling them
"ordinary translatable wording" would be true only in a sense that produces
the wrong answer.

They are worth naming because of what §3 shows next. The byte-identical string
`6/{v1}/{v2}:{v3}PM` appears three times in AramcoWorld and gets **three
different outcomes**:

| ref | outcome | output |
|---|---|---|
| p4#16 | translated | `6/{v1}/{v2}:{v3}下午` |
| p7#20 | translated, differently | `6/{v1}/{v2}:下午{v3}` |
| p6#13 (row 4 above) | returned unchanged | `6/{v1}/{v2}:{v3}PM` |

The pipeline is not consistent about this string, and none of its three
behaviours is desirable.
That is a finding about placeholder-only paragraphs reaching the translator at
all, not about the rotation, threshold or prompt work this batch was sorting.

### The finding this batch did not go looking for

Rows 7–18 are twelve paragraphs on **bull-zh page 4**, a run whose direction is
**Chinese to English**. Their inputs are English. Their outputs are Chinese.

The translator was handed English words in a run targeting English and
translated them into the source language. The ledger records a translation
because a translation did occur; the detector reports Han residue in an
English-target document because that is exactly what is there. **Both
instruments are right, and the thing they disagree about is the defect.**

All twelve carry `repair_refusal = orphan_residue_threshold_not_met` and
`layout_label = fallback_line`, and they sit together on one page — the word
cloud graphic of an IAEA bulletin contents page. This is a single localized
failure, not a scattered one, which is why it did not surface as a rate
anywhere in b11.12.

`A\B` totals: **16 `detection_false_positive`** (6 here, plus the 10
`correct_preservation` of §2, which are false positives from the detector's
side of the same disagreement), **12 `ledger_wrong`**, **3 `undecided`**.

## 5. Recommended order for the four follow-ups

1. ~~**`rotated`, and the rotation lane behind it.**~~ **Done — see §7.** It
   was the largest unexamined class in A (16 of 58) and the only one whose
   size was not known to be real. The re-run confirms all 16 from the IL's own
   flag. The rotation lane now has a measured 16-of-58 population behind it and
   is the largest confirmed class in A.

2. **`residue_min_script_chars_into_zh`.** Deferred by plan 10 pending this
   triage, and the triage now argues for it from a direction the plan did not
   anticipate: the threshold's problem is not that it is too high but that
   16 of 31 `A\B` records are the detector firing on correctly preserved
   personal names, credits and URLs. A threshold change alone will not fix
   that — the detection surface needs to stop counting a preserved proper noun
   as residue. Do it after 1, because 1 may move the same records.

3. **fixed artwork (task D).** Independent of everything above, and task D's
   argument stands on its own: the blind spot is the whole `rendered_text`
   detection surface, not residue. Its priority does not depend on this
   triage, and the probe is read-only.

4. **prompt work.** Last. The nine genuine misses in §4 are not prompt
   failures — eight were never submitted at all, refused upstream by
   `orphan_layout_label_not_allowed` and `formula_paragraph`. No prompt can
   translate a paragraph the translator never sees.

### One item this list did not have room for, and should

The twelve wrong-direction translations on bull-zh p4 (§3) are not on plan
10's list of four because nothing before this triage knew they existed. They
are a concrete, localized, reproducible defect in which the pipeline
translated English into Chinese during a Chinese-to-English run. They belong
above item 4 and arguably above item 2; where exactly is a judgement about
whether a word-cloud graphic on one contents page is worth a lane of its own,
which is a question for whoever sets the next batch.

## 7. The rotation re-run — reading the real `vertical`

§4 left the 16 `rotated` records unmeasured and §5 made measuring them item 1.
This section is that measurement.

The six samples were re-run whole with the detector from `b11.14-rotation-
evidence`, so their artifacts carry `vertical` in issue evidence. Artifacts
under `examples/output/b11.14/full/`, gitignored, so the numbers are reproduced
here.

**Every sample ran on a fully warm translation cache** — 56/56, 76/76, 63/63,
62/62, 22/22, 61/61 — so not one translator request was issued and the
translations are the b11.12 translations exactly. The only variable between the
two censuses is the field the detector now reports. 468 s wall for all six.

### What moved

Across all 149 records, exactly two fields differ from b11.12:

| field | records changed |
|---|---|
| `vertical` | 58 — every population A record, each of which has an issue to carry it |
| `criterion` | 16 — switched from `aspect_ratio<0.25` to `paragraph.vertical` |

Nothing else. `|A|=58`, `|B|=90`, `|A∩B|=27`, `|S|=28`, 149 records, and every
population A category count is unchanged:

| category | b11.12 | b11.14 |
|---|---|---|
| `translated_with_residue` | 18 | 18 |
| `rotated` | 16 | 16 |
| `returned_unchanged` | 13 | 13 |
| `never_submitted` | 10 | 10 |
| `below_length_floor` | 1 | 1 |

**Records that changed category: zero.**

### The threshold was right every time it was asked

Of the 58 records that now carry the flag, 16 say vertical and 42 say
horizontal. Set against the 0.25 box-shape threshold:

```
carry the flag                         58
  flag and threshold agree             58
  flag and threshold disagree           0

vertical=False AND aspect_ratio<0.25    0   (the veto never fired)
```

The veto that task B built — evidence overruling shape — is correct in
principle and turned out to be unnecessary in fact on this corpus. Every record
the threshold ever decided, it decided the same way the paragraph itself would
have. That is a real result about the threshold and not a null one: `0.25` was
a guess when it was declared, and it is now a guess that has been checked
against 58 known answers and got 58 of them right.

Two `rotated` records are still decided by shape: `Courier-zh p3#2` and
`WIPO-zh p5#1`. Both are population B only — from the coverage ledger, with no
issue behind them (`issue_id: null`, `residue_basis: excerpt_estimate`) and so
nothing to carry a flag. This is exactly the case B.3 kept the threshold for,
and it is the whole of that case: 2 records of 149.

### What this settles, and what it does not

Settles: the rotated class is real. Sixteen of the 58 population A records are
paragraphs the IL itself marks as set along the vertical axis. It is the
largest confirmed class in A — larger than the 12 wrong-direction bull-zh
translations of §3 and the 9 genuine misses of §4 — and the rotation lane
(`magazine_rotated_lane`) now has a measured population behind it rather than
an inferred one.

Does not settle: whether those 16 paragraphs *should* be translated, or what a
rotation lane should do with them. This measured how many there are, not what
to do about them. Nothing in this section touched a switch or a threshold.

**Revised answer to the batch's second question:** of the 58 population A
records, **zero** changed classification once the evidence arrived. The 16
rotated records did not move — they were reclassified from a guess that
happened to be right to a fact, which changes what is known about them without
changing where they sit.
