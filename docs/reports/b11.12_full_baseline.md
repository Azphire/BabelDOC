# b11.12 — untranslated text over whole documents

Baseline commit `eed1dc3` (`b11.11-short-unit-switch`), branch
`migration/minimal-v0.6.4`. Census instrument committed at `faad061`, before
any whole document was run.

This was a measurement. No pipeline code, no `configs/` parameter, no
`sample_matrix.json` and no `expectations/*.json` was changed. The only code
added is `tools/residue_census.py`, which is read-only.

Run artifacts live under `examples/output/b11.12/full/`, which is gitignored;
every number below is therefore reproduced in this document rather than
referenced.

---

## 0. What was counted, and why two populations

Every residue count published before this one was taken over a three-to-five
page window chosen from the truth nodes of `corpus/*.json` — pages picked
because they were already known to be interesting. This census takes the count
over whole documents instead.

Three sets are counted:

| set | definition |
|---|---|
| **A** | `untranslated_residue` issues in `issues.after.json` — what the detector saw |
| **B** | `demo_coverage.report.json` items with `final_status = untranslated` — what the ledger holds was never translated |
| **S** | admitted units in `short_unit.report.json` — paragraphs the short-unit lane took |

`S` is carried because two of the plan's calibration refs (Courier `p3#1`,
`p4#0`) are in neither A nor B: they are absent *because* that lane worked.
Counting them is how the lane's contribution stays visible. A and B counts are
unaffected by its presence.

**Directional incomparability.** `configs/detectors.json` declares a residue
floor of 12 characters into Chinese and **1** into English, with shares 0.6 and
0.4. Population A is therefore an order of magnitude easier to enter in the
zh→en direction. Every A aggregate below is split by direction and must not be
summed across it. B is unaffected and is the only cross-direction comparison
made here. This asymmetry is recorded, not adjusted.

## 5. B \ A — the detector's blind spot

63 records, and the finding of this census.

| direction | n | page_policy | below_threshold | unclassified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| en→zh | 20 | 0 | 20 | 0 |
| zh→en | 43 | 0 | 17 | 26 |

Per sample: Courier-en 4, AramcoWorld-en-v2 0, FD-en-v2 16, Courier-zh 5,
WIPO-zh 8, bull-zh 30.

### 5.1 `page_policy` is structurally unreachable

Zero records, and zero is the only value it can take. All fifteen page types in
`configs/page_types.json` declare `translate: true`, and `residue.py:59` reads
the flag as `view.flag(TRANSLATE_POLICY_FLAG, True)` — a page whose kind the
vocabulary does not know defaults to being scanned. **There is no page, of any
kind, that the residue detector skips wholesale.** The `continue` on
`residue.py:60` is dead under the shipped configuration.

### 5.2 `below_threshold` is the floor, not the share

37 records. Which limb fired:

| direction | `residue_chars < floor` | `residue_ratio < share` |
|---|---|---|
| en→zh | 20 (floor 12) | 0 |
| zh→en | 15 (floor 1) | 2 (share 0.4) |

**The character floor accounts for 35 of 37; the ratio for 2.** Into Chinese
the floor of 12 alone explains every single one. Whatever is done about the
threshold asymmetry, it is `residue_min_script_chars_into_zh` that is doing the
hiding, not `residue_min_ratio_into_zh`.

### 5.3 A third blind-spot path exists, and it is fixed artwork

26 records could not be attributed to either declared cause. All 26 are
bull-zh; all 26 are `fallback_line`; all 26 carry
`source_text_sha256 == sha256("")` in the coverage ledger and an empty excerpt
in `indent_policy.report.json`.

`bull-zh`'s `line_split.report.json` holds exactly **26** `fixed_artwork`
entries, with reasons `embedded_figure_xobject` and
`adjacent_spread_figure_overlay`. **Matching by source box, all 26 unclassified
records match a fixed-artwork entry — 26 of 26, exact.** No other sample in the
set has any fixed artwork at all, which is why this path has never been seen.

The mechanism is in the code. `base.rendered_text` returns `""` when
`line_split.is_fixed_artwork` holds, and `residue.py:63` then does
`if not text: continue`. So a paragraph inside an embedded figure XObject is
skipped by the detector while the coverage ledger still counts it as an
untranslated source. That is a third `continue`, at a line the plan's
vocabulary did not name.

Per plan §8 the vocabulary was not amended after the fact. These records are
reported as `unclassified`, which is what the closed vocabulary requires, and
the cause is named here instead.

## 10. Verifier results

### `verify_minimal_pdf.py` — en→zh only

Run for the three en→zh samples over all their pages. The three zh→en samples
were **skipped**: the verifier hardcodes `HAN` as the target script at `:42`,
`--allow-untranslated` disables the check at `:140`, and `:176`'s
`_require(translated is not allow_untranslated, ...)` makes that switch mutually
exclusive with `translation_performed = True`. No zh→en invocation passes. This
is the known gap from plan §3.1 and was not fixed.

| sample | result |
|---|---|
| AramcoWorld-en-v2 | `MINIMAL_PDF_VALID`, 9 pages |
| FD-en-v2 | `MINIMAL_PDF_VALID`, 9 pages |
| Courier-en | **fail** — `fixed assets drifted` |

Courier-en's failure is pre-existing, not caused by this run: `fixed.holds` is
`false` with `drift_count: 2` in both the fix0829 baseline and the b11.12 run,
identically.

### `verify_magazine_demo.py` — all five registered samples at their frozen `stage_pages`

`stage_pages` were not modified; whole-document runs were obtained from the
command line, per plan §3.2.

**All five fail.** Four fail identically with
`unadjudicated chain skip: ('', None)`; FD-en-v2 fails earlier with
`unadjudicated detector chain: ('p6#6', 'p6#7')`.

The first is a **regression on this branch, not an effect of running whole
documents.** The control is decisive: the same verifier, same expectations,
same 1–8 page scope, run against `examples/output/fix0829/Courier-en`, returns
`status: pass` on all seven checks.

Diagnosis. The short-unit lane writes a skip record into
`chain_translation.report.json` with `chain_id: ""`, `chain_index: null`,
`taken_by: "short_unit"`. `verify_magazine_demo.py:450` requires every skip key
to match a `(chain_id, order)` pair derived from the expectations' chain
members, and `("", None)` never can. The correlation is exact across all six
samples:

| sample | short units admitted | anonymous chain skips |
|---|---|---|
| Courier-en | 1 | 1 |
| AramcoWorld-en-v2 | 1 | 1 |
| FD-en-v2 | 5 | 5 |
| bull-zh | 8 | 8 |
| WIPO-zh | 1 | 1 |
| Courier-zh | 12 | 12 |

FD's different error *is* a whole-document artifact: `p6#6`/`p6#7` is a chain on
page 6, outside FD's declared `stage_pages` of 3, 8, 9. The verifier adjudicates
detector chains against the expectations regardless of `--pages`, so a run
covering undeclared pages will always raise it. This is the inverse of the
hazard plan §3.1 warned about, and it means **`verify_magazine_demo` cannot
validate a whole-document run of a sample whose `stage_pages` are a subset**,
whatever `--pages` is passed.

Neither was fixed, per plan §8.

## 11. The three questions (plan §9)

### 1. How much of the whole document is rotated text?

**About a quarter, not a half.** Population A: 11/39 = 28% (en→zh), 5/19 = 26%
(zh→en). Population B: 11/40 = 28% (en→zh), 7/50 = 14% (zh→en).

The three-page windows put it at 50–75% of A. The window over-represented it by
roughly two-fold. Rotation remains the single largest category of A in
Courier-en (3/5), Courier-zh (5/6) and AramcoWorld (3/11 tied), but it does not
dominate: in FD's 23 A records it is 5, against 16 that were submitted to the
translator and came back unchanged or were never submitted at all.

**The rotated lane should not automatically be the next priority.** On the
whole-document distribution, `never_submitted` (10) + `returned_unchanged` (13)
= 23 of 58 A records outweigh rotation's 16.

A note on how this was measured. The IL's `vertical` flag is never serialised
into any artifact, so `rotated` was decided on the box aspect ratio alone,
against the `0.25` declared for this census. Independent corroboration: the two
records the pipeline itself refused with `repair_refusal = vertical_paragraph`
have aspect ratios 0.071 and 0.047 — both far inside the threshold, both
classified `rotated`. That is the only place the pipeline's own rotation
judgement surfaces in artifacts, and it agrees.

### 2. How large is the blind spot, and what causes it?

**63 records, versus 58 the detector reported — the blind spot is larger than
the finding.** Causes:

- `page_policy`: **0, and structurally unreachable.** Every page type declares
  `translate: true` and unknown kinds default to scanned. The page-type
  translation policy is not the problem and cannot be.
- `below_threshold`: **37**, of which **35 are the character floor** and 2 the
  ratio. Into Chinese, `residue_min_script_chars_into_zh = 12` explains all 20.
- A third path, unnamed by the plan: **26 fixed-artwork paragraphs**, skipped
  because `rendered_text` returns `""` for them.

So the answer is neither of the two the plan offered exclusively. The fixable
surface splits: the threshold work is one change to
`configs/detectors.json`, and the fixed-artwork work is a decision about
whether a paragraph inside a figure XObject should be scanned at all — which is
a question about `line_split.is_fixed_artwork`, not about detector thresholds.

### 3. How many unclassified, and do they merge?

**26, and yes — into one category, with proof.** All 26 match a
`line_split.fixed_artwork` entry by source box, 26 of 26, and the code path that
produces them is identified. A name for it in a future vocabulary would be
`fixed_artwork_not_scanned`.

Worth stating precisely, because the two axes are easy to conflate: across the
149 distinct records the census produced, **not one fell into the
`unclassified` category.** The seven-category vocabulary of plan §4.1 covered
every record in all three populations. The 26 unclassified sit on the other
axis — the blind-spot causes of §4.3 — where the plan offered two options and
reality had three.

**No second census round is needed.** The category vocabulary held completely,
and the one gap resolves to a single identified mechanism. The next step can be
repair work.

## 12. What this changes about what to do next

Ranked by what the distribution actually supports:

1. **The `never_submitted` / `returned_unchanged` pair (23 of 58 A records).**
   Larger than rotation and never separated before. `returned_unchanged` in
   particular means the translator was given the text and handed it back
   verbatim — 13 records, 8 of them in FD alone. That is a prompt or a
   model-behaviour question, not a layout question.
2. **`residue_min_script_chars_into_zh = 12`.** Sole cause of all 20 en→zh
   blind-spot records. A single parameter.
3. **Fixed artwork (26 records).** Confined to one sample today, but that sample
   is the only one in the set with any fixed artwork — the true prevalence is
   unmeasured because the corpus barely contains the case.
4. **Rotation (18 distinct records, 16 of them in A).** Still real, now
   correctly sized at about a quarter rather than a half.
5. **`verify_magazine_demo`'s short-unit skip.** Not a residue matter, but it
   currently fails all five registered samples and the fix0829 control proves it
   is new. It blocks any future use of that verifier as a gate.

Two of these — items 2 and 5 — are single-line changes whose diagnosis is
complete. Item 1 is the one with the most residue behind it and the least prior
investigation.
